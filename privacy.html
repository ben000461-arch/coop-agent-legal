Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

co|op Agent (“we”, “the assistant”) is an AI assistant that people use through Telegram. This policy explains what information it uses, why, and the control you have. We keep it short on purpose.

What we access

  • Your messages to the assistant in Telegram, so it can understand and answer them.
  • Google account (only if you connect it). Gmail: permission to create email drafts at your request. We do not send email, and we do not read, search or list your inbox. Google Calendar: read-only access to your events, to answer questions like “what’s on my calendar today?”. We never change or delete events.
  • GitHub (only if you connect it). Access to the specific repositories you choose, to read project files and open pull requests at your request.
  • Files the assistant creates for you (spreadsheets, text files, saved code changes) and basic usage counts used to apply fair-use limits.

How we use it

  • Only to do what you ask, when you ask. Connected accounts are used on request, not in the background.
  • Anything that changes something (a file, a draft, a pull request) is shown to you first and needs your explicit yes.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles.

Where it is processed and stored

  • Messages are understood by an AI model that runs on the computer hosting the assistant.
  • For code changes only, your request and the one file being edited may be sent to a cloud AI provider. Businesses are asked for permission before this happens the first time.
  • Information from your Google account is processed on the computer hosting the assistant and is not sent to cloud AI providers.
  • Your connection credentials are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Recent chat context is kept in memory for about two hours to make follow-up questions work, and is erased when the assistant restarts.

Google API data

The assistant’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you request, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features or comply with law, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people except with your consent, for security, or to comply with law.

Your control

  • Send /disconnect in the chat to remove a connection and delete the stored credentials.
  • You can also revoke access any time in your Google Account permissions or in your GitHub settings under Applications.
  • To have your saved files and records deleted, email us at the address below.

Children

The assistant is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
