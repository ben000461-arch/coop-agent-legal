About your agent

Your agent

What it is

A private AI assistant you text on Telegram. It helps with everyday work: emails, spreadsheets, notes, your calendar and small code changes.

What it can do

  • Save email drafts in Gmail. It never sends email and never reads your inbox.
  • View your Google Calendar to answer questions and, only after your yes, add events. It never changes or deletes events you already have.
  • Create and edit spreadsheets and text files, and keep notes.
  • Prepare code changes in the GitHub projects you choose, as a pull request, only after you say yes.

How it works

It shows you what it is about to do and waits for your yes before it changes anything. Everything it does is listed on the Logs page, and the Tools page shows which tools it used.

    Help

    How this site and your agent work.

    Around this site

    • Chat: your home screen. Web chat is coming soon, so for now you talk to your agent in Telegram.
    • Get started: the first-time setup, step by step.
    • Agent: what your agent is and what it can do.
    • Tools: the apps your agent can use. Open one to see what it did and how to connect or disconnect it.
    • Logs: charts of what your agent handled, plus every action, when it happened and how it went.

    How your agent is linked to you

    When you sign up, we review your request. Once you’re approved, Get started gives you a personal link made just for your login. Tap it in Telegram, press Start, and your agent ties itself to this login. Your agent belongs to one Telegram chat, and this website login is linked to that same chat, so you only ever see your own agent’s activity. Nobody else can open your chat or your logs.

    Link made by mistake, or not you? Send /unlink to your agent. If anything looks wrong, email cooptech126@gmail.com.

    Things you can say

    • “what’s on my calendar today?”
    • “schedule a meeting with Tom tomorrow at 3pm” (it asks before adding it)
    • “build an agent”, a helper that runs on a schedule and texts you
    • “draft an email to Sam about Friday”
    • “make a spreadsheet of my expenses”
    • “note buy milk”, then “show my notes”
    • “what pull requests are open?”

    Just talk normally. If it isn’t sure what you mean, it asks instead of guessing. Before it changes anything, it shows you and waits for your yes.

    Commands

    /help
    What the agent can do. Add a question, like /help what should I do next.
    /connect google
    Link Gmail drafts and your calendar.
    /connect github
    Link the code projects you choose.
    /agents
    Build and manage your agents (up to 3). Each runs on a schedule you choose and only reads and reports; it can’t change anything by itself.
    /timezone
    Set the time zone your calendar and agents use.
    /connections
    See what’s connected.
    /disconnect google
    Remove a connection (also github).
    /code in myapp, fix the crash in server.js
    Ask for a code change. It shows you the change first.
    /changes
    List saved code changes you can still turn into a pull request.
    /model
    Choose which AI writes your code.
    /unlink
    Disconnect the website login from your agent. You can link again from Get started.
    /instructions
    Tell the agent about your business and how you like it to talk.

    Good to know

    • It only saves email drafts. You send them yourself.
    • It adds a calendar event only after you say yes, and it never edits or deletes the ones you already have. (Google words that permission as “view and edit events”.)
    • Code changes go onto a new branch as a pull request, and only after you say yes.
    • Pasted passwords and keys are refused, so don’t send them in the chat.
